Who We Are

Background & Identity

Company Background

OPTIMA TOTAL SOLUTION was founded by Muhammad Hafiz Bin Ridzuan, whose experience covers more than ten years in building maintenance, six years as an electrical contractor and two years of cabling work in oil and gas.

Today the company brings electrical, plumbing, and networking and CCTV work together under one name, so customers can deal with one team for all three.
